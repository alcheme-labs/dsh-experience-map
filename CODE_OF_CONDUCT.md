# Code of Conduct

We want participation in Experience Map to be safe, constructive, and technically honest.

Contributors must be respectful, welcome good-faith questions, critique ideas rather than people, protect private data, disclose conflicts of interest, and report results without hiding failures or overstating evidence. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of credentials or Session content, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the project or its community. Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel; do not expose affected people in a public issue. Maintainers will consider context, severity, safety, and proportionality when responding.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
